Help & Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How fast can Elite Workforce deploy manpower for site projects?
We maintain a live pool of pre-vetted technicians and workers across UAE emirates. Standard deployment takes between 24 to 48 hours after requirement verification.
2. Are all manpower suppliers on this platform licensed?
Yes, all suppliers listed on our Public Board undergo trade license authentication and document checks to ensure 100% legal compliance with UAE labor standards.
3. How can Azad Visa workers get daily or hourly site leads?
Azad Visa holders can fill out the quick registration form by clicking the "Azad Visa Worker" button. Once verified, their skills are published on the public board for direct company contact.
4. Is there any fee for companies to post job requirements?
No, posting requirements on Elite Workforce Solutions is completely free for registered UAE companies and hiring partners.
